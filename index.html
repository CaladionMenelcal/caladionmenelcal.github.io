✦ The Diary ✦

Day 6 · Morning

The morning mist still clings to the valley as I wake in the storage hut. Straw pricks through the thin blanket — but I've grown used to it. The work at Maron's bakery has become my anchor now.

Sixth morning. The gift of listening.
Day 5 · Morning

Waking early, the air crisp. Maron's bakery calls — flour dust settles everywhere, on my braids, on the wooden table, on yesterday's bread. But there's something satisfying about the routine now.

Fifth morning. Bread and belonging.
Day 4 · Morning

Walked to the creek early. The water so cold it hurts — but the laundry needed washing. Sat by the water and watched the current carry leaves downstream. Met Sera by the creek. She never says much, but her presence is enough.

Fourth morning. The forest listens.
Day 3 · Afternoon

Climbed the old windmill today. The view — mountains stretching into mist, the village tiny below. Creek Grandmother gave me her ocarina. "When you're ready," she said. I'm not sure what for, but I hold it carefully.

Third afternoon. The gift.
Day 2 · Noon

Morning at the bakery — the heat of the ovens, the smell of bread rising. Maron shows me the rhythm: knead, rest, shape, wait. My arms ache but there's joy in the work. Lily brought me water without asking. A small kindness.

Second day. Flour dust and gratitude.
Day 1 · Evening

First night in Kakariko. The stone cabin is cold but the fire warms the room. I sit near the crackling wood, watching the light dance on ancient stones. The windmill turns slowly in the dark. This is my home now.

First night. Arrival.
